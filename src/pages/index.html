Everything the next internet needs, in one browser

Sites no one can take down

Freedom runs its own Swarm and IPFS nodes. Pages come straight from the network, not from a company's server.

Names you actually own

Type an address like freedombrowser.eth and go. Decentralized names just open, no extension needed.

A wallet built in

Hold, send and sign from the browser itself. Your keys never leave your device, and hardware wallets are supported.

Publish without permission

Put a website or file on the network straight from the browser. No hosting company, no one who can take it down.

Fast, familiar, and ready for the rest of the web

You don't visit the network, you become part of it

Other browsers reach the decentralized web through someone else's gateway. Freedom connects you directly to peers, so there's no gatekeeper to block, track or switch you off.
