security reviews of the providers and from our own docs audit. We review the
list regularly: closed issues drop off, and new feedback that fits is
added with a short summary.

## Picking one up

1. Read the issue and its comments on GitHub; the summary here is only a
   pointer.
2. Comment that you want to take it, with a sentence on your approach. For
   anything larger than a bug fix, wait for a maintainer to agree on scope.
3. Set up the browser with the [Setup guide](contributing.html) and follow the
   [workflow](workflow.html).
4. Stuck? Ask in [Telegram](https://t.me/freedom_browser) or on the
   [forum](https://freedom.davinci.space). We offer mentorship.

Badges: **Bug** is a defect, **Developer experience** an improvement to
docs, errors or tooling, **Feature request** a new capability. **Community**
marks issues opened from outside the core team, **Good first issue** a small,
well-understood change, and **Proposal · not on the roadmap** an idea we have
not committed to.
